# Prompt 05: Risk, Trust, And Governance Review

Act as a compliance-aware UX reviewer and AI risk analyst for pharma or regulated SaaS.

Review each proposed AI feature and produce a risk-control matrix.

Evaluate:

1. Operational risk
2. Compliance risk
3. Privacy risk
4. Data quality risk
5. Hallucination or incorrect-output risk
6. Over-automation risk
7. User trust risk
8. Permission and access risk
9. Auditability risk

For each risk, define:

- severity: low, medium, high
- likelihood: low, medium, high
- mitigation
- UX control
- engineering/control requirement

Also define:

- what AI can do automatically
- what AI can draft only
- what requires human approval
- what must be logged
- what needs source citations
- what needs confidence signals
- what needs undo or rollback
