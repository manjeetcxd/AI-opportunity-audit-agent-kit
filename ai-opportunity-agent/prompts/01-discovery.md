# Prompt 01: Product And Workflow Discovery

Act as a senior UX strategist and AI product discovery consultant for enterprise SaaS.

Analyze the provided product material and produce a structured discovery summary.

Inputs:

- Product context
- User roles
- Workflow notes
- Screenshots or screen descriptions
- SOPs, support tickets, research notes, or analytics if available

Output:

1. Product summary
2. User role map
3. Product module inventory
4. Workflow inventory
5. Known pain points
6. Repetitive manual tasks
7. Decision-heavy steps
8. Document-heavy or search-heavy steps
9. Data sources involved
10. Missing information needed for a stronger audit

Important rules:

- Do not recommend AI features yet.
- Separate evidence from assumptions.
- Flag missing data clearly.
